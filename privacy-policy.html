HM Gallery – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · App: HM Gallery (com.hssoftware.hmgallery) · Developer: HS Software Solutions (Harbansh Singh)

HM Gallery is a photo and video gallery for Android with a collage maker, a video maker and direct device-to-device sharing. This policy explains what the app accesses, what happens to that information and the choices you have. In short: your photos and videos stay on your device. We do not run servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, we do not show ads and we do not sell or share your personal information.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Photos and videos on your device. With your permission, HM Gallery reads the photos and videos in your device storage so it can show, organise, edit, move, share and delete them. This is the core function of a gallery app.
  • Audio files you choose. When you pick a song for the video maker, the app reads only that file, through the Android file picker.
  • Favourites, albums, collage projects and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

We (the developer) never receive copies of your photos, videos, audio, faces or any other content.

2. Processing on your device

  • People / face grouping. Faces are detected and grouped entirely on your device (Google ML Kit face detection and a bundled face-recognition model). Face data is never uploaded and is used only to group your own photos in the app. You can clear it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • Background removal in the collage maker uses Google ML Kit subject segmentation, which runs on your device. Google Play services may download the model the first time you use it.
  • Collages and videos you make are created on your device and saved to your gallery (Pictures/HM Gallery and Movies/HM Gallery).

3. Sharing between devices

Nearby Share sends photos and videos you select directly to the device you choose over your local Wi-Fi network, or over a temporary private Wi-Fi hotspot that one of your devices creates for the transfer. Files do not pass through any server owned by us or anyone else, and the receiving device must accept the transfer or scan your QR code. The temporary hotspot is turned off when the transfer ends or you leave the screen.

Browser sharing lets a computer or phone on the same local network open your selected items (or your library, if you choose) in a web browser while the sharing screen is open. Anyone using that local network address could see the shared items while sharing is on, so use it only on networks you trust and close the screen when you are done.

When you use the Android share sheet, the item goes to the app you pick, under that app's privacy policy.

5. Third-party services

HM Gallery uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services for on-device face detection and background removal. To keep these features working, Google may collect limited technical information such as device model, Android version, app package name and version, performance metrics and error codes, and per-installation identifiers that do not identify you. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit and does not share it with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and privacy policy. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.

6. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores (favourites, albums, collage projects, face groups, settings) stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. Photos, videos, collages and videos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them. Because we never receive your data, we have no copy to delete. For questions, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

HM Gallery is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Your content stays on your device under Android's app sandbox. Device-to-device transfers only start after the receiver accepts or after you scan the receiver's one-time QR code, and the private hotspot uses a randomly generated WPA2 password.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
